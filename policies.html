02 / REFUND POLICY

A fair digital-access policy.

Digital access begins after a completed payment. If you were charged twice, received the wrong product, or cannot access the purchased content because of a verified technical problem we cannot resolve, request help through the PayPal transaction.

Request window

Submit a request within 7 calendar days of purchase and include the transaction ID and checkout email. We review requests individually and may confirm account activity or attempt to fix access before issuing a refund.

Limits

A change of mind after substantial use of the protected library is not normally refundable. Nothing in this policy limits non-waivable consumer rights that apply where you live.

03 / PURCHASE TERMS

Personal access, plainly stated.

What you receive

A completed purchase grants the buyer a limited, personal, non-transferable right to stream the purchased LegitBodyFix program or session through the buyer library. It does not transfer ownership of the videos or related materials.

Account responsibility

Access is linked to the checkout email. Do not share sign-in links, bypass access controls, copy, redistribute, resell, publicly display, or make the protected content available to another person.

Movement education

LegitBodyFix provides general movement education, not medical diagnosis, emergency care, physical therapy, or individualized medical treatment. Stop if an activity causes concerning symptoms and seek qualified professional guidance when appropriate.

Availability and changes

We may maintain, replace, or improve site features and delivery systems. We aim to preserve access to purchased content, but temporary interruptions may occur for maintenance, security, or circumstances outside reasonable control.

04 / PRIVACY

Only what the service needs.

Information processed

LegitBodyFix processes the email used for checkout and sign-in, purchased product, payment amount and currency, PayPal order and capture identifiers, access status, and relevant timestamps. Security and hosting providers may also process technical request information such as IP address, browser details, and service logs.

What we do not store

LegitBodyFix does not receive or store your full card number or card security code. PayPal processes payment details under its own privacy terms.

Why information is used

  • Complete and verify purchases.
  • Grant and protect access to paid content.
  • Send secure authentication messages.
  • Prevent abuse, investigate failures, and provide support.
  • Meet accounting, tax, fraud-prevention, and legal obligations.

Service providers

The service currently uses PayPal for payments, Supabase for authentication and purchase access records, Cloudflare for protected video delivery, and Vercel for website hosting.

Retention and requests

Purchase and access records are retained while needed to provide lifetime access and to meet security, accounting, dispute, and legal requirements. To ask about a record connected to a purchase, use the seller-contact option on the PayPal transaction so ownership can be verified.
